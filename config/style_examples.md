# Style Examples

> **Starter examples written by the AI setup, not by TektiZen.** Replace them with real posts in the exact voice you want, either your own or ones you admire (rewritten, not copied). 5–10 examples work best. The Malay Localizer imitates this file more than anything else.
>
> Approved posts are also added to the Localizer's context automatically, so the voice improves as you approve and edit content.

## Example 1: Educational (single post)

Ramai guna ChatGPT macam Google. Taip soalan, dapat jawapan, habis.

Padahal benda paling berguna dia ialah bila korang bagi konteks:

1. Korang siapa (admin HR, pelajar diploma, owner kedai online)
2. Output tu untuk siapa
3. Format yang korang nak

Cuba sekali, beza dia memang ketara.

Korang selalu guna AI untuk buat apa?
