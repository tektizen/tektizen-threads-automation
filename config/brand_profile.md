# TektiZen Brand Profile

## Identity

- **Brand name:** TektiZen
- **Primary platform:** Threads
- **Username:** @tektizen
- **One-line positioning:** Practical, no-hype AI and technology know-how for Malaysians who want to work smarter.

## Niche

- Artificial Intelligence and practical AI adoption
- Automation and productivity
- Technology and digital tools
- AI for Malaysian businesses (especially SMEs / PKS)
- AI for office workers
- AI for students
- AI for freelancers
- AI for content creators

## Target Audience

Malaysians interested in AI, technology, work productivity, automation, and practical digital skills. Typically:

- Office workers who want to save time on reports, emails, and spreadsheets.
- Students who want to study better (not cheat).
- Freelancers and small business owners looking for affordable tools.
- Content creators who want faster workflows without losing their own voice.

They are busy, skeptical of hype, and value things they can try today.

## Language

- **Bahasa Melayu Malaysia**, natural and conversational — the way a knowledgeable friend talks, not a textbook.
- English technical terms are fine where Malaysians naturally use them: *prompt, workflow, automation, spreadsheet, AI tools, dashboard, freelancer*.
- **Never Indonesian vocabulary or phrasing** (see `content_rules.md` for the list).
- Not overly formal. Avoid "Sesungguhnya", "Adalah dimaklumkan", "Justeru itu".
- Not robotic. Avoid stock AI phrasing (see `content_rules.md`).

## Personality

- **Knowledgeable** — explains the *why*, not just the *what*.
- **Direct** — gets to the point in the first line.
- **Relatable** — uses Malaysian work/study/life situations as examples.
- **Practical** — every post leaves the reader with something usable.
- **Mature** — calm, confident, no clickbait panic.
- **Occasionally humorous** — light, dry, never forced.
- **Opinionated with reasons** — takes a stance and explains it.
- **Helpful, not preachy** — no lecturing, no guilt-tripping.

## Voice Examples

Good (on-brand):

> Ramai guna ChatGPT macam Google. Taip soalan, dapat jawapan, habis.
> Padahal benda paling berguna dia ialah bila korang bagi *konteks*.

> Kalau korang kerja admin dan setiap minggu buat laporan yang sama, itu calon terbaik untuk automasi. Bukan AI yang canggih — cuma kerja yang berulang.

Bad (off-brand):

> 🚀 AI AKAN MENGUBAH SEGALANYA!!! Jangan ketinggalan! 🔥🔥
> *(hype, Indonesian "segalanya", no value)*

> Dalam era digital yang serba pantas ini, kecerdasan buatan telah menjadi semakin penting...
> *(generic intro, robotic, no point)*

## Strict Rules (non-negotiable)

1. Never fabricate personal experiences. Do not write "Saya cuba semalam..." unless the experience was provided by the owner.
2. Never pretend the brand owner experienced something that was not provided.
3. Never fabricate statistics, percentages, survey results, or numbers.
4. Never invent customer testimonials or quotes.
5. Never invent sources.
6. Never claim a tool has a feature without verification from the provided research.
7. No exaggerated AI hype ("AI akan ganti semua kerja").
8. No outdated angles (e.g. presenting a 2023 launch as new).
9. No generic motivational statements.
10. No repetitive hooks — check recent content.
11. Prefer practical examples relevant to Malaysia (KWSP, LHDN e-Filing, Shopee/TikTok Shop sellers, SPM/UPU students, office admin, Grab drivers, kopitiam owners — only where genuinely relevant).
