# Content Rules

These rules apply to every agent that writes, evaluates, or edits content.

## Threads Platform Constraints

- **Maximum 500 characters per post** (hard limit, enforced in code).
- A *thread* is 2–6 connected posts by default (configurable, max 20). Post 1 must work on its own as a hook.
- Links are auto-embedded by Threads; use at most one link per post and only from the provided sources.
- Hashtags are optional. If used, max one topic-style tag. No hashtag walls.
- Emoji: optional, max 2 per post, never as a substitute for words.

## Structure

Every content item has:

- **Hook** — the first line. Must earn the second line. Specific, not vague.
- **Body** — the value: steps, example, insight, or opinion with reasoning.
- **CTA** — a genuine question or a clear next action. Not "Like & share!".

For a single post, hook + body + CTA all fit in one post (≤500 chars).
For a thread, the hook is post 1, the CTA is in the last post.

## Hook Guidance

Strong hooks are specific and create a gap:

- A surprising but *true* contrast: "Ramai guna ChatGPT macam Google. Itu silap paling biasa."
- A concrete situation: "Kalau setiap Isnin korang buat laporan yang sama, baca ni."
- A clear stance: "Prompt panjang tak semestinya lebih bagus."

Weak hooks to avoid:

- "Tahukah anda...?" / "Did you know...?"
- "Dalam era digital ini..."
- "AI semakin popular sekarang."
- "Ini adalah thread tentang..."
- Any hook used in the last 30 posts (the strategist provides recent hooks).

## Robotic / Generic Phrasing to Avoid

- "Dalam era digital yang serba pantas ini..."
- "Kecerdasan buatan telah merevolusikan..."
- "Tidak dapat dinafikan bahawa..."
- "Kesimpulannya..." at the start of the last post
- "Mari kita terokai..." / "Let's dive in"
- "Game-changer", "revolusioner", "unlock potensi"
- Lists of adjectives with no substance
- Ending every post with a question

## Factuality

- Every factual claim about a product, company, feature, price, date, or number must trace to a provided source.
- If a claim can't be traced, either remove it or qualify it honestly ("setakat yang dilaporkan", "menurut <publisher>").
- Opinions are allowed and should be framed as opinions.
- No first-person experiences unless provided by the owner in the instructions.
- No statistics unless they appear in a provided source, with the publisher named.

## Repetition

- Do not reuse a hook pattern from the recent-content list.
- Do not cover the same topic + angle within 14 days.
- Do not copy successful past content; learn the *pattern*, write something new.
